# Code of conduct

pii-guard follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good faith, give and take criticism of the work
rather than of the person, and don't harass anyone, in public or in private. This applies in
issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent the project.

One rule specific to this project: **never post someone's real personal data**, not as an
example, not partly redacted, and not to demonstrate a bug. Use fictional data.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately, through GitHub's
[private reporting](https://github.com/watt-mind/pii-guard/security/advisories/new) form
with "Code of conduct" in the title. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers who don't
follow this code may face the same consequences as anyone else, as set out in the Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
