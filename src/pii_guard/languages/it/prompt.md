- Italian names do not decline: report every occurrence as written ("Giulia Ricci", "la
  Ricci", "secondo Marchetti"). A surname often stands alone after an article ("la Caruso",
  "della Barbieri"): report only the surname, not the article.
- Do not include titles or forms of address ("sig.", "sig.ra", "dott.", "dott.ssa", "avv.",
  "ing.", "prof.", "geom."), also when they are elided onto the name ("dell'avv. Rinaldi"):
  report "Rinaldi".
- Addresses are written "Via dei Mille 27, 40121 Bologna (BO)" (also "Viale", "Piazza",
  "Corso", "Largo", "Vicolo", "C.so", "P.zza"); report the street, house number, CAP, town and
  province together. A five-digit CAP before a town ("00184 Roma") is an address.
- The codice fiscale (16 letters and digits, "C.F."), the partita IVA (11 digits, "P.IVA"),
  the carta d'identità (CIE, "CA00000AA"), passport ("passaporto"), driving-licence
  ("patente") and tessera sanitaria numbers are ID_DOCUMENT.
- Do not report generic roles ("il Venditore", "l'Acquirente", "il Conduttore", "il
  Locatore", "il Cliente", "il Ricorrente").
