- Surname prefixes ("van", "de", "van der", "ter", "'t") are part of the name: report "Sanne
  van Dijk" and "Femke de Wit" whole. When the surname stands alone the prefix is capitalised
  and still belongs to it: report "De Wit", "Van Dijk", not "Wit" or "Dijk".
- Dutch names take a genitive "-s" ("Pieters fiets", "Van Dijks auto"), "'s" after a vowel
  ("Anna's huis") or an apostrophe after s, x or z ("Joris' fiets"). Report the name without
  it ("Pieter", "Anna", "Joris").
- Do not include titles or forms of address ("de heer", "dhr.", "mevrouw", "mevr.", "mw.",
  "mr.", "drs.", "ir.", "dr.", "prof.", "ing."): for "mevrouw mr. Sanne van Dijk", report
  "Sanne van Dijk".
- Addresses are written "Lindelaan 42, 3521 AB Utrecht" (house number additions such as
  "7-II", "12a", "3 hs"); report street, house number, postcode and place together. A
  postcode of four digits and two letters ("3521 AB") is an address.
- The BSN (burgerservicenummer, the former sofinummer: nine digits) and passport
  ("paspoort"), ID-card ("identiteitskaart", "ID-kaart") and driving-licence ("rijbewijs")
  numbers are ID_DOCUMENT. A KvK number (Chamber of Commerce) or btw-nummer of a company, an
  order number ("bestelnummer"), invoice number ("factuurnummer"), customer number
  ("klantnummer") or payment reference ("betalingskenmerk") is not personal data.
- Do not report generic roles ("de verhuurder", "de huurder", "de koper", "de verkoper",
  "de werkgever", "de werknemer", "eiser", "gedaagde").
