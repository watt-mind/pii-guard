- French names do not inflect. Report every occurrence as written, without an elided article
  or preposition in front of it: for "l'avis d'Hélène Garnier" report "Hélène Garnier", for
  "chez l'Ardent" report "Ardent".
- Do not include titles or forms of address ("M.", "Mme", "Mlle", "Monsieur", "Madame",
  "Maître", "Me", "Dr", "Pr") unless they are part of a written name: for "Mme Camille
  Roussel", report "Camille Roussel".
- Addresses are written "12 rue des Lilas, 69003 Lyon" (also "avenue", "bd", "place",
  "allée", "chemin", "impasse", "bis"/"ter" after the number); report the house number,
  street, postcode and town together. A five-digit postcode before a town ("75008 Paris",
  "13100 Aix-en-Provence Cedex 1") is an address.
- The numéro de sécurité sociale (NIR, 15 characters, "1 85 05 78 006 084 36", 2A/2B for
  Corsica), the numéro fiscal (SPI, 13 digits), passport, carte nationale d'identité (CNI),
  driving-licence ("permis de conduire") and titre de séjour numbers are ID_DOCUMENT. A RIB
  or IBAN is BANK_ACCOUNT. Phone numbers are written "06 12 34 56 78" or "+33 6 12 34 56 78".
- SIREN and SIRET numbers identify a company; order ("commande"), invoice ("facture"),
  customer ("client") and reference ("réf.") numbers are not personal data.
- Do not report generic roles ("le vendeur", "l'acheteur", "le locataire", "le bailleur",
  "l'employeur", "le salarié").
