- German names can take a genitive "-s" ("Annas Wohnung", "Müllers Auto") or an apostrophe
  after s, ß, x or z ("Hans' Fahrrad"). Report the name without it ("Anna", "Müller").
- Do not include titles or forms of address ("Herr", "Frau", "Dr.", "Prof.") unless they are
  part of a written name: for "Frau Dr. Erika Beispiel", report "Erika Beispiel".
- Addresses are written "Musterstraße 12, 10115 Berlin"; report street, house number,
  postcode and place together. A postcode before a place name ("10115 Berlin") is an address.
- The tax identification number (Steuer-ID, Steueridentifikationsnummer, IdNr), the social
  insurance number (Sozialversicherungsnummer, Rentenversicherungsnummer), ID card
  (Personalausweis) and passport numbers are ID_DOCUMENT.
- Do not report generic roles ("der Mieter", "die Vermieterin", "der Käufer").
