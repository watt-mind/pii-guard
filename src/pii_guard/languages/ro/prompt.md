- Romanian names decline in the genitive/dative. A feminine given name in "-a" changes its
  ending ("Maria" -> "Mariei", "Ioana" -> "Ioanei", "Monica" -> "Monicăi"); a surname keeps
  its form after "lui" ("lui Popescu") or takes "-lui"/"-ului" ("Popescului"). Report the
  name exactly as it is written in the text, in its inflected form ("Mariei Popescu"); the
  nominative ("Maria Popescu") is its lemma.
- Do not include titles or forms of address ("domnul", "doamna", "dl.", "dna.", "dr.",
  "av.", "ing.", "prof.") or the proclitic "lui": for "doamnei Ioana Popescu", report
  "Ioana Popescu"; for "lui Andrei Ionescu", report "Andrei Ionescu".
- Addresses are written "Strada Teilor nr. 15, bl. A3, sc. 2, ap. 7, 400123 Cluj-Napoca"
  (also "Str.", "Bd."/"Bulevardul", "Calea", "Șoseaua", "Aleea", "sector 3", "jud. Cluj");
  report the street, numbers, postcode (six digits) and town together.
- The CNP (cod numeric personal, 13 digits), the identity card series and number ("CI seria
  RX nr. 123456", "buletin"), passport ("pașaport") and driving-licence ("permis de
  conducere") numbers and a CIF/CUI (tax code, "RO" + digits) are ID_DOCUMENT.
- Diacritics may be written with a comma (ș, ț) or a cedilla (ş, ţ), or left out ("Stefan",
  "Bucuresti"); they are the same names and places.
- Do not report generic roles ("Vânzătorul", "Cumpărătorul", "Locatorul", "Chiriașul",
  "Angajatorul", "Clientul") or company names with "SRL"/"SA" as persons.
