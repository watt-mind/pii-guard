- Spanish names do not decline. People usually carry two surnames ("Javier Molina Ruiz",
  "Marina Ortega Gil"); report the given name and both surnames together, and a surname
  used on its own ("Ortega", "Domínguez") as a PERSON too.
- Do not include titles or forms of address ("Sr.", "Sra.", "Srta.", "D.", "Dña.", "don",
  "doña", "Dr.", "Dra.", "Lic."): for "la Sra. Dña. Marina Ortega Gil", report
  "Marina Ortega Gil". "de" inside a name belongs to it ("María de la Paz", "Luis de Castro").
- Addresses are written "Calle del Olmo 14, 3.º B, 28012 Madrid" (also "C/", "Avda.",
  "Plaza", "Paseo", "Ctra."); report the street, number, floor and door, postcode and town
  together. A five-digit postcode before a town ("46021 Valencia") is an address.
- The DNI / NIF (eight digits and a letter, "12345678Z"), the NIE ("X1234567L"), the
  passport ("pasaporte"), social security ("número de la Seguridad Social", NUSS) and
  driving-licence ("permiso de conducir") numbers are ID_DOCUMENT. A company's CIF
  ("B12345674") belongs to the company: report it as ID_DOCUMENT only when it is labelled.
- Order, invoice, customer and reference numbers ("pedido", "factura", "cliente",
  "referencia", "expediente") are not personal data.
- Do not report generic roles ("el vendedor", "el arrendatario", "el comprador",
  "el cliente", "el titular").
