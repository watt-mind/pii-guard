- Polish names decline in seven cases, and the stem often changes ("Jan Kowalski", "Jana
  Kowalskiego", "Annie Nowak", "z Anną Nowak"). Report every occurrence exactly as it is
  written in the text, in its inflected form.
- Do not include titles or forms of address ("Pan", "Pani", "dr", "mgr", "inż.", "prof.")
  unless they are part of a written name: for "Pani mgr Anna Przykładowa", report
  "Anna Przykładowa".
- Addresses are written "ul. Kwiatowa 12/5, 00-950 Warszawa" (also "al.", "os.", "pl.");
  report the street, house and flat number, postcode and town together. A postcode in the
  form NN-NNN before a town ("31-008 Kraków") is an address.
- The PESEL (11 digits), NIP (tax number, "123-456-32-18"), REGON, ID card ("dowód
  osobisty", three letters and six digits, "seria i numer"), passport (two letters and seven
  digits) and driving-licence ("prawo jazdy") numbers are ID_DOCUMENT.
- Do not report generic roles ("Sprzedający", "Najemca", "Pracodawca", "Zleceniobiorca").
